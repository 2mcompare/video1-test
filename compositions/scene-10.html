Final Verdict

For Frontend

DeepSeek V4 Flash

Reliable, multi-constraint code that follows complex briefs without going off the rails. Use it.

For Creative

Big Pickle

Personality + technical requirements — holds both registers simultaneously. Exceptional.

For Small Models

MiMo v2.5

7B parameters, 7.5/10. Remarkable. Punching way above its weight class.

Avoid for Frontend

Nemotron & Gemma 4

Neither is ready for complex single-file frontend work. Gemma may surprise elsewhere — but not frontend.
