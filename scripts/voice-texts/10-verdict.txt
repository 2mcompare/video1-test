Alright. Final verdict. Here is the honest answer to — which model should you actually use? For frontend coding — DeepSeek V4 Flash. It is not even close. If you need reliable, multi-constraint code that follows a complex brief without going off the rails, this is your model. Use it. For creative briefs — when the project has personality, humor, or a specific brand voice alongside technical requirements — Big Pickle is exceptional. It held both registers at the same time, which is genuinely hard to do. If you are resource constrained and need a small model — MiMo. Seven billion parameters, seven point five out of ten. That is remarkable. And for this specific kind of complex single-file frontend work — skip Nemotron and skip Gemma four. Neither is ready for this use case in its current form. Gemma might surprise you on other tasks but frontend code is not it right now.
